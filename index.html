01 · The Studio

Stories become worlds.

Kamora Studios is an independent animation and entertainment brand developing original characters, stories and cinematic experiences.

We believe animation can be funny, emotional, strange, beautiful and unforgettable—all at once. Kamora exists to turn bold ideas into characters audiences care about and worlds they want to return to.

Our first chapter is focused on original short-form animation, world-building and experimental storytelling, with room to grow into series, films, music, interactive experiences and merchandise.

“Every world starts with an idea.” Kamora creative principle
